Assetto Corsa EVO Fuel Calculator

A simple and customizable fuel calculator for Assetto Corsa EVO.
The calculator comes preloaded with GT3 cars, circuits and fuel per lap for convenience, so you can get started right away without having to enter everything manually.

You can fully customize the settings to match your own data, including fuel consumption, lap time, reserve fuel, pitstop(s) and race conditions.

The goal is to make fuel planning quick, straightforward, and reliable, helping you spend less time calculating and more time racing.

Feel free to use it, customize it, and adapt it to your own needs.
