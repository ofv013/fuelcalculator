Assetto Corsa EVO Fuel Calculator

A simple and customizable fuel calculator for Assetto Corsa EVO.
The calculator comes preloaded with GT3 cars and circuits for convenience, so you can get started right away without having to enter everything manually.

You can fully customize the settings to match your own data, including fuel consumption, lap time, car selection, circuit, and race conditions.

Easy to use for planning fuel requirements before a race
The goal is to make fuel planning quick, straightforward, and reliable, helping you spend less time calculating and more time racing.

Feel free to use it, customize it, and adapt it to your own needs.
